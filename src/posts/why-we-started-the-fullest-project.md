Welcome to The Fullest Project!  This is the brain child of two high school best friends who went to college, got married, built careers, started families, became caregivers of neurodivergent children, and wanted to find a way to make this life a little easier for others like us.



We are both originally from the Northwest and have now landed in Portland, OR and Northern Virginia.  A few years ago, when Erin came to visit me, we were sitting at a restaurant on our way to the beach for a much-needed mom's weekend away.  During the natural course of our ever topic jumping conversation, Erin asked a question that changed our trajectory forever. She asked: If you could change one thing or make one thing easier about being a caregiver of a disabled child, what would it be?  



I sat back in my chair to really reflect on that.  There are MANY challenges that caregivers of disabled children have to deal with - from navigating benefit programs, to advocating for school supports, to understanding government programs and waivers, to living the real impacts of state and federal legislation.  But all of those problems have better educated and equipped organizations to provide support than we could. 



Then it hit me - as caregivers ourselves, we know firsthand how isolating and overwhelming it can feel to navigate the world of disability resources. You hear about a great program from another parent at therapy. You stumble across a foundation through a random Google search at 2 AM. You find out about equipment loaner programs only because someone in your support group mentioned them.  We happened to have built (or in my case found through a chance encounter in a doctor's waiting room) a great community that has helped us in our journeys - but what about those who don't have that?  Why should their kids not have the same opportunities just because their caregiver didn't know what they didn't know, and didn't have anyone that could tell them otherwise?  



This conversation planted a seed that unfortunately stayed dormant for a few years due to the logistical challenge it would be to create something so robust.  Then, one fateful night in 2026, I was talking to my husband (who is an AI tech expert) during our Friday night date night and he said "Send me your brainstorm ideas for the Fullest Project and let's see what AI can do."  Within 24 hours we had a live website, AI agents that could support the manual work that made something like this impossible before, and the excitement of a long percolating dream finally becoming a real possibility. 



We have spent this year refining the website, cataloging resources, building out the back-end support, and working towards our 501c3 designation.  That and dreaming what this could become and how many families and caregivers and children this could help.  The best part is that the more we share The Fullest Project, the more community we build, the more resources we capture.  I personally have learned SO much this year while searching for resources and topic areas to add to the site, and am excited about what more I will learn from each of you who contribute your knowledge to the cause.  



To all those caregivers out there - we see you.  We know how hard this life can be and want to make at least one part of it a little less overwhelming.  We hope The Fullest Project can be a part of your journey as you discover one day at a time, how your child can live their lives to the fullest. 

\-Nicole
